BISCUIT GATE Privacy Policy

Effective date
November 8, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you play BISCUIT GATE, an iOS puzzle game designed for children ages 5 and up, visit this policy page, or contact us for support.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress, plush toy collection, and settings on your device so you can continue playing where you left off. Using Delete App in iOS removes the app and its local save data from that device. Offload App keeps the app’s documents and data on the device. Any copies in existing device backups may remain until those backups are deleted.

You can play without creating an account with us. We do not receive your saved progress, collection, or settings from the game. The game contains no ads or third-party analytics and does not track players across other companies’ apps or websites.

In-app purchases

All 20 stages in CHICK are free. A single, non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks access to the other 240 stages.

The game uses Apple’s StoreKit 2 to retrieve product information, process purchases, check whether the full game has been purchased, and restore purchases. Apple handles your Apple Account and payment information. The game uses the purchase status provided by StoreKit to determine whether to unlock the additional stages. We do not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details through the game.

For details about Apple’s handling of this information, see App Store & Privacy.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children for their name, email address, or other personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. A parent or guardian can use the contact details below to ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of personal information a child may have sent to us by email.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and resolve your request. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and meet any applicable legal obligations.
